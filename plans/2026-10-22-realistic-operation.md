# Realistic mining operation: staged delivery

The persistent goal is a direct-control gold-mining game with realistic digging and fluids, buildable pads, mud, shaker decks, sluice buildup, settling pools, and high quality graphics. The goal remains incomplete until the complete operation works and is verified. The water-rendering checkpoint `71a195b` is the starting point for these stages, not the completion criterion.

Stage 1 is verified in the [22 October earthwork report](../docs/playtests/2026-10-22-earthworks.md). The first conservative pump handoff and safe final-render teardown are verified in the [intake report](../docs/playtests/2026-10-22-intake.md). Terrain bed exchange now uses immutable geometry, stored transfers, physical-time deposition and saved numerical feedback, documented in the [bed-exchange model](../docs/physics/terrain-bed-exchange.md) and [settling model](../docs/physics/terrain-settling.md). These are verified prerequisites, not a constructed pond or calibrated soil model. Stages 2–6 and the remaining prerequisites below stay open. Closing these bounded stage issues does not close this full objective.

Stage 1 replaces a verified bug: holding cut with a stationary bucket mined 0.28 m³ and 4.77 g of gold. It also replaces four complete terrain clones and a 16,384-cell scan per hand/machine cut with bounded local column updates. The old domain path skipped deposited gravel. New excavation uses the same layer/trace-gold and water-depth accounting for both tools.

The initial soil-resistance model is an explicitly limited level-ground passive-pressure approximation: integrate `Kp*rho*g*z + 2*c*sqrt(Kp)` over depth and blade width. It is not the complete Reece bucket equation, a calibrated soil continuum, or multibody hydraulics. The 25 cm heightfield cannot yet represent fine trenches, overhangs or excavated failure wedges. Those are Stage 2 requirements, not exceptions to the final objective. The machine force envelope uses an 8-ton-class reference; the modeled linkage and tool-power assumption still need calibration. Settling's 1.5-second stability clock is likewise a retained proxy; physical compaction and armouring require a better model.

Compaction must distinguish grain mass, bulk volume, porosity and pore water: the current bulk-volume ledger is insufficient when void volume changes. Stage 2 must conserve grain mass and total pore/surface water while allowing compaction, swelling and expelled water. The [mass foundation](../docs/physics/material-mass.md) now distinguishes conserved reference bulk inventory from actual grain solids and keeps emitted DEM mass consistent. Finite [wet-material custody](../docs/physics/soil-water.md) now carries column pore water through excavation, hauling, deposition and actual FLIP feed, with version 4 persistence. Dynamic infiltration, vertical/lateral pore-water flow, pore pressure, wet failure-block advection and variable compaction remain unimplemented. The initial saturation and drained-column response are stated approximations; they do not close Stage 2. Pads require finite cut/fill and bearing/support; a cosmetic flattened mesh does not satisfy Stage 3.

The first earthwork stage also exposed global-solver prerequisites. Conservative collapse and clean-source dilution are implemented. Saved per-class rounding feedback holds the final 20,000-stage residuals below 0.051 ppm of initial mobile inventory, without widening their one-ppm limit. Porosity, pore water, hindered settling, flocculation and calibration remain open; these controls do not validate a constructed settling pool. The first [pump handoff](../docs/physics/intake.md) now carries local sediment/gold into saved inlet custody and actual DEM parcels, with source/capacity starvation. `sluice-intake-solids` remains open for physical hose/pressure behavior, coarse tracer calibration and the complete constructed pond circuit.

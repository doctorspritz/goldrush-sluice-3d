# Realistic mining operation: staged delivery

The persistent goal is a direct-control gold-mining game with realistic digging and fluids, buildable pads, mud, shaker decks, sluice buildup, settling pools, and high quality graphics. The goal remains incomplete until the complete operation works and is verified. The water-rendering checkpoint `71a195b` is the starting point for these stages, not the completion criterion.

Stage 1 is verified in the [22 October earthwork report](../docs/playtests/2026-10-22-earthworks.md). The first conservative pump handoff and safe final-render teardown are verified in the [intake report](../docs/playtests/2026-10-22-intake.md). Terrain bed exchange now uses immutable geometry, stored transfers, physical-time deposition and saved numerical feedback, documented in the [bed-exchange model](../docs/physics/terrain-bed-exchange.md) and [settling model](../docs/physics/terrain-settling.md). These are verified prerequisites, not a constructed pond or calibrated soil model. Stages 2–6 and the remaining prerequisites below stay open. Closing these bounded stage issues does not close this full objective.

| Stage | Deliverable | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Swept earthwork | Actual bucket floor/tooth motion cuts finite layered terrain; load, capacity and a documented force/power approximation limit motion. Shared local column removal includes deposited gravel. | Idle tool cannot mine; cut geometry and timestep convergence; bedrock/passive contact; force/power/capacity limits; material/gold/water conservation; old/new save continuity; real scoop/dump control replay and native visual evidence. |
| 2. Local soil and machine contact | Finer physical excavation geometry, failure wedges/spoil movement, saturation and compaction; bucket/boom contact and track support/traction/tipping. | Resolution and timestep convergence; known slope/soil cases; tracks lose support; no tunnelling, free cuts, duplicated spoil or unsupported soil-strength claims. |
| 3. Constructed pads and ponds | The player grades, fills and compacts support pads; excavates pools; builds finite berms and spillways. | Surveyed grades and support limits; cut/fill volumes and gold reconcile; water responds to actual geometry; construction and drainage survive save/restore. No free flattening or prescribed pond water. |
| 4. Wash plant | Moving, wet shaker deck with geometric apertures; finite oversize/undersize streams; two-way sluice phase exchange, bed loading/scour and washout. | Single-grain contact and aperture controls before beds; documented stroke/frequency/PSD/water; conservative component transfers; closed momentum budgets and packing convergence; actual cleanup assays and holdout operating cases. |
| 5. Water circuit and operation | Settling by size/residence, recirculation, intake starvation, overflow, maintenance and long-duration operation. | Full water/soil/gold inventory and boundary/source budgets; actual constructed pool retention and sediment capture; flow/feed/particle-size variations and live save/restore. |
| 6. Graphics and playability | Credible terrain and equipment, stable water reconstruction, legible mud/sediment, responsive first/third-person controls and clear operator feedback throughout the loop. | Native digging/driving/construction/washing/cleanup replay, multi-view motion review, persistence and measured FPS plus simulation/wall multiplier. A passing shader test does not prove visual quality or real-time physics. |

These stages may overlap when a shared foundation requires it. Existing `sluice-machine-dynamics`, `sluice-phase-coupling`, `sluice-calibrate`, `sluice-shaker-calibrate`, `sluice-realtime`, `sluice-water-polish` and `sluice-environment-art` remain part of this goal. `sluice-realistic-operation` tracks the complete objective; closing a stage never closes the epic automatically.

Stage 1 replaces a verified bug: holding cut with a stationary bucket mined 0.28 m³ and 4.77 g of gold. It also replaces four complete terrain clones and a 16,384-cell scan per hand/machine cut with bounded local column updates. The old domain path skipped deposited gravel. New excavation uses the same layer/trace-gold and water-depth accounting for both tools.

The initial soil-resistance model is an explicitly limited level-ground passive-pressure approximation: integrate `Kp*rho*g*z + 2*c*sqrt(Kp)` over depth and blade width. It is not the complete Reece bucket equation, a calibrated soil continuum, or multibody hydraulics. The 25 cm heightfield cannot yet represent fine trenches, overhangs or excavated failure wedges. Those are Stage 2 requirements, not exceptions to the final objective. The machine force envelope uses an 8-ton-class reference; the modeled linkage and tool-power assumption still need calibration. Settling's 1.5-second stability clock is likewise a retained proxy; physical compaction and armouring require a better model.

Compaction must distinguish grain mass, bulk volume, porosity and pore water: the current bulk-volume ledger is insufficient when void volume changes. Stage 2 must conserve grain mass and total pore/surface water while allowing compaction, swelling and expelled water. The [mass foundation](../docs/physics/material-mass.md) now distinguishes conserved reference bulk inventory from actual grain solids and keeps emitted DEM mass consistent. Finite [wet-material custody](../docs/physics/soil-water.md) now carries column pore water through excavation, hauling, deposition and actual FLIP feed, with version 4 persistence. Dynamic infiltration, vertical/lateral pore-water flow, pore pressure, wet failure-block advection and variable compaction remain unimplemented. The initial saturation and drained-column response are stated approximations; they do not close Stage 2. Pads require finite cut/fill and bearing/support; a cosmetic flattened mesh does not satisfy Stage 3.

The first earthwork stage also exposed global-solver prerequisites. Conservative collapse and clean-source dilution are implemented. The snapshot/settling work now has immutable slope reads, stored-delta reconciliation, moving-tile protection and physical-time deposition/scour controls. Saved per-class rounding feedback holds the final 20,000-stage residuals below 0.051 ppm of initial mobile inventory, without widening their one-ppm limit. The former 1 mm deposit gate, calm boost and frame-based age are replaced. `sluice-thin-film-sediment` tracks continuous sediment stranding and rewetting across pond margins; deposition now applies below 1 mm, while rough-bed scour retains that applicability floor. Porosity, pore water, hindered settling, flocculation and calibration remain open; these controls do not validate a constructed settling pool. The first [pump handoff](../docs/physics/intake.md) now carries local sediment/gold into saved inlet custody and actual DEM parcels, with source/capacity starvation. `sluice-intake-solids` remains open for physical hose/pressure behavior, substep-local gold transport and the complete constructed pond circuit. The pump-off dig/delivery gate and a controlled intake pulse do not establish the complete circuit or plant stage. The historical 20 October full-shift replay predates swept excavation; the current small feed takes roughly 97 simulated minutes for a 0.58 m³ load.

The subgeometry material foundation now extends deposition to every positive water depth and preserves actual precision stock through GPU readback, cuts, fills and version 5 saves; see [the model and limits](../docs/physics/subgrid-bed.md). This is distinct from signed numerical compensation. `sluice-bed-precision` tracks this bounded prerequisite. `sluice-thin-film-sediment` remains responsible for full drying/rewetting and sloping shoreline convergence, and soil-water flow, physical compaction, arbitrary fill layers and the complete operation remain open.

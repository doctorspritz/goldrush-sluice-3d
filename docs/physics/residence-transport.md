Terrain sediment now settles during each hydraulic transport substep. Previously it advected for a complete terrain tick before deposition, even when the mixed-column settling time `h/ws` was much shorter. A 0.8 mm paydirt control has a settling timescale of about 2.55 ms; the old 50 ms update delivered about 14.8 times the independently integrated receiving-cell material. Residence-weighted transport reduces that case to about 0.43% error without adding water substeps.

The physical source law remains `dC/dt = -ws*C/h`, derived from the [noncohesive deposition flux](https://www.hec.usace.army.mil/confluence/rasdocs/d2sd/ras2dsedtr/6.6/model-description/deposition). The four inherited class diameters and terminal velocities remain. This implementation is a first-order explicit finite-volume approximation with analytically integrated reaction weights, not the full HEC transport solver, a calibrated bedload model or a dense-slurry law.

For a hydraulic substep of duration `dt`, the local reaction coefficient is frozen at its arithmetic mean old/new water depth: `r_i = ws*dt/max((h_old+h_new)/2, epsilon)`. This is an approximation to changing-depth residence; refinement is still required for rapidly varying hydraulics. Define `E(r)=exp(-r)` and `phi(r)=(1-exp(-r))/r`, with `phi(0)=1` and a small-r polynomial to avoid cancellation. A donor with old material `M`, outgoing water fraction `f` and reaction `r` retains mobile material `M*(1-f)*E(r)`. Its interface/export material is `M*f*phi(r)`. The rest deposits before its face crossing or while remaining in the donor.

Material arriving from a donor with reaction `r_d` retains the convolution weight `K(r_d,r_i)=integral_0^1 exp(-r_d*t-r_i*(1-t)) dt`. The stable form is `exp(-min(r_d,r_i))*phi(abs(r_d-r_i))`. Thus incoming mobile material is `C_d*q*K`, and incoming deposition is `C_d*q*(phi(r_d)-K)`. This accounts for time after arrival instead of applying a complete extra interval of settlement to every incoming parcel. When both reactions are equal, mobile transport is the original donor-cell update multiplied by the exact exponential decay. At zero reaction it reduces to the existing upwind water-face transport. At very fast reaction, most material deposits locally while the finite material that crosses a face retains its proper shortened residence. No unbounded reaction microstep loop is used.

The water solver still owns its hydrostatic wet/dry reconstruction, donor-limited shared face volumes, Manning friction and CFL count. No water-grid/timestep reduction is made. Deposition changes geology between those existing water substeps, so coupled water trajectories can change. Scour remains a separate full-tick Shields/hardness stage; the isolated bed-stage API remains an analytic mixed-column control. Pressure, FLIP/APIC, DEM and riffle equations are unchanged.

Interface material is quantized identically at donor and receiver. Per-cell bed receipt uses old mobile material plus incoming transfers, less outgoing/exported transfers and the actually represented new concentration. Integer-anchored signed high/low sums and fused product residuals preserve this balance without subtracting a deposit from a large geometric layer. Counted positive [bed precision stock](subgrid-bed.md) receives it. Existing signed numerical feedback carries subprecision negative roundoff into the next exchange; it is not counted as another material store. Zero elapsed time is an exact identity, and a quiet unchanged-depth column updates concentration directly with its analytic decay.

The f64 [gold tracer](terrain-gold.md) follows the same departure and arrival residence weights. The two extra event records now bracket the subsequent scour. It therefore does not deposit the same gold a second time. Obsolete initial bed-copy arrays were removed. Local precision stock, global/export custody, transient event reconstruction and version 5 saves remain; loading prior snapshots preserves their recorded inventories, and subsequent steps use the new dynamics.

Validation includes an independent RK4 continuous balance driven by actual GPU water faces; manufactured translated/decaying Gaussian and integrated bed profiles; a separate exact Poisson donor-cell reference for time refinement on a fixed grid; cross-depth temporal quadrature for every class, including zero reaction; quiet-depth/substep/zero-time controls; and existing dry/wet, open-boundary, moving-tile, restore and provenance controls. Manufactured transport prescribes its hydraulic field and is distinct from a complete runoff or constructed-pool convergence proof. The original one-ppm mobile-material limits remain unchanged.

At 25/12.5/6.25 cm manufactured grids, normalized mobile-profile errors fall 12.15% → 6.57% → 3.41% and bed-profile errors fall 4.90% → 2.53% → 1.28%. On a fixed 12.5 cm grid with 0.125/0.0625/0.03125 s steps, errors against the exact semidiscrete reference fall 4.13% → 2.00% → 0.983% for mobile material and 1.41% → 0.733% → 0.374% for the bed. These are discretization controls; the playable terrain remains 25 cm. Quiet-column concentration error is below 0.709 ppm and material error below 0.039 ppm. The existing long isolated exchange controls remain separate from moving-plume controls.

Continuous sloping wet/dry shoreline convergence, submillimetre rough-bed entrainment, grain immersion/bedload, hindered settling, cohesive flocculation, arbitrary strata and moving gold-bearing failure blocks remain open. Dynamic infiltration, pore pressure, physical compaction, constructed pads/ponds, shaker decks, full phase coupling and empirical recovery calibration remain required by the [complete operation plan](../../plans/2026-10-22-realistic-operation.md).

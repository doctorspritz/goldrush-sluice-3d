`--new-shift --articulated-machine --active-soil` prepares a finite bank for interactive **drained, frictional soil contact**. Move the machine's actual links with R/F, T/G and Y/H. The soil bodies, articulated machine, substrate and equipment share one contact solve. Soil reacts through its mass, inertia, normal contacts and friction; the old prescribed cutting-force transaction is not used in this mode. This is an opt-in Stage 2 experiment, not a calibrated digging machine or completed realistic operation.

The initial bank is prepared before time starts. The current exterior-mounted bucket fixture activates 24 nodes covering the whole tool footprint: about 270 litres represented by 560 convex clods. The earlier ShellV1 checkpoint used a different 135 litre bank with 304 clods; its yield is not a matched comparison with this fixture. The source is withdrawn from actual site stock, including its material classes, gold and pore water. Preparation is an initial condition, not credited operator excavation work. Subsequent input never injects material or teleports bodies. Ordinary shifts retain their existing cutting model.

Activation clones the candidate site, removes selected finite nodal columns and tessellates the entire support of those nodes. Each prism fills the gap between the old and lowered terrain triangles, using the same diagonal as the pinned heightfield collider. Boundary wedges remain finite bodies; the lowered substrate remains a real collider. Subdivision refines contact geometry without changing the removed inventory. The nodal 25 cm geology and linear heightfield shape are still the source geometry. Removed column constituents are mixed within each clod; this does not resolve finer geological layering.

For a subtriangle of area A and mean barycentric weights w_i, its material share is `sum(M_i * A * w_i / CELL²)`. Vertical subdivisions split that share again. All incident triangles together recover each interior node's actual removed stock. Canonical f64 dry mass, reference bulk volume, gold and pore water remain separate from f32 collision geometry. The measured geometry-volume discrepancy is retained explicitly; an activation that the heightfield cannot resolve is refused without changing the site. Capacity is 2,048 bodies, checked before committing a new activation.

Uniform prism mass moments come from three tetrahedral integrals. Collider inertia and the rendered mesh use the same stored vertices. Every clod has an independent orientation, linear velocity and angular velocity. Canonical wet mass includes retained pore water. Normal contacts are unilateral; restitution is zero. Friction uses a declared contact coefficient derived from the material mixture's 28/30/36 degree soil/sand/gravel assumptions. These are **contact assumptions**, not evidence that the aggregate's macroscopic failure angle or strength is calibrated. Cohesion, strain softening, dilatancy and compaction laws remain required.

The existing contact engine's radial clipping of a coupled two-axis friction trial fails the inclined-block reference: the triangular block develops large spin and slides too fast. More PGS/TGS iterations did not correct it. The new per-world option projects onto the Coulomb disk in the contact effective-mass metric. It minimizes `0.5 (lambda-old)^T K (lambda-old) + v^T (lambda-old)`, subject to `|lambda| <= mu*N`. On the boundary, `(K + alpha*I) lambda = K*old-v`. A positive-mass matrix gives a monotone scalar radius and an analytic upper bracket. Twenty-four bounded bisections retain the feasible side; a final norm cap handles float roundoff. Exact zero-radius SIMD batches now return the unique zero impulse before matrix inversion or root finding. A mixed batch containing only frictionless and sticking lanes masks the zero-radius lanes without starting the root loop. The caller still applies the complete impulse delta, including removal of an old warm-start impulse. Any batch containing positive-radius sliding retains all 24 original bisections and its feasible-side result. Mixed-lane arithmetic controls retain the previous implementation as a differential reference; the independent energy/slip tests remain the physical oracle. Articulated contacts calculate the cross term from both bodies' full generalized inverse-mass Jacobians, rather than the free inertia of the bucket alone.

This implementation follows the Coulomb bound and dissipation direction described in [Drake's physical friction model](https://drake.mit.edu/doxygen_cxx/group__friction__model.html); the particular projection and its tests are implemented here. [Servin, Berglund and Nystedt](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40323-021-00196-3) explain why moving soil needs inertia and dynamically coupled machine reactions, and why coarse soil contact parameters require bulk calibration. Their calibrated multiscale model and reported accuracy are not claimed for these prisms.

The exact Rapier 0.36.1 archive is vendored with its Cargo.lock checksum and original-file hashes in [UPSTREAM.json](../../vendor/rapier3d/UPSTREAM.json). The shared registry is untouched. `maximum_dissipation_friction` defaults false, and existing ordinary-world contact arithmetic remains in the false branch. Only an active-soil world enables the correction. This opt-in design replaced a rejected proposal to change the default shared friction law. Existing ordinary and articulated-without-soil control traces are checked byte for byte against fingerprinted pre-change traces, reproduced again with their preserved statically linked registry-backed executable. Subsequent builds replaced the cached baseline library, so that changed file is not reused as the reference. Broader adoption still requires its own contact, control and load validation.

The mechanical clock remains 240 Hz by default, with four TGS stages and sixteen PGS iterations. Each mechanical boundary rebuilds the contact world; cached shapes and local principal inertia are derived only from immutable geometry/material. This gives the checked saved continuation no hidden warm-start history, but is expensive for hundreds of bodies. The [exact-friction runtime follow-up](../playtests/2026-10-23-soil-friction.md) reduces fixed CPU bank replay time by about 19% with matching complete saved snapshots; real-time throughput remains unresolved. The native renderer draws the actual prism meshes and solved poses. A matched comparison showed that the earlier mesh was already visible; its layout was not a proven cause of missing soil. UVs and tangents now support metre-scale rough soil detail using the existing terrain texture handles. This adds no collision relief, geometry, material stock or additional texture images. An independent closed-hull test checks its volume and outward faces. Transforms currently follow worker snapshots, so smoother machine/soil presentation and real-time throughput remain open. It does not substitute decorative spheres or uncounted soil piles.

Version 9 preserves the previous floor-proxy moving-soil model. New articulated machines use the [exterior-mounted hollow shell](bucket-shell.md) and version 11, while version 10 retains its original ShellV1 geometry, preserving moving-soil geometry, finite materials, moments, dynamics and diagnostics. Ordinary and old articulated/spatial-payload saves remain versions 6/7/8/9. Current readers accept 2–11 and reject mismatched version/model tags, invalid prism/mass state and unsupported wet contact. Moving pore water remains counted in the whole-water ledger. No adaptive ordinary excavation, settled-body redeposition or completed scoop/dump yield is claimed.

Surface water touching these clods is explicitly unsupported: moving-solid displacement and effective-stress forces have not been coupled to terrain flow. The dry-contact guard refuses that state; it is not a wet-contact solver. FLIP/APIC, riffles, terrain flow and weighted in-sluice DEM retain their original equations and physical resolution. Separate combined-pipeline checks exercise water flow and moving soil on a dry part of the claim, not immersed mud.

New articulated machines now use the [shared physical/visual bucket shell](bucket-shell.md), cheek plates and flush-entry teeth. Independent dry clods can be scooped, retained through a lift and discharged by opening the bucket; they remain physical bodies rather than scalar carried payload. Old saved machines retain their floor-plate proxy. Realistic bulk yield, cohesive failure, wet mud and continuous local activation remain necessary. The soil tests cover independent prism moments, finite inventories, a free-system tool impact, known block friction, physical-step refinement, deterministic v9 continuation and an operator stroke against a stationary control. None establishes bulk digging convergence, soil calibration or full-machine energy closure. Ground-contact runs report signed coupled passivity residuals; only explicitly free cases claim closed momentum. Stages 2–6 of the [operation plan](../../plans/2026-10-22-realistic-operation.md) remain open. The [native checkpoint evidence](../playtests/2026-10-23-active-soil.md) records visible motion, exact full-body restore, the unchanged ordinary traces and the failed real-time gates.


The MountedShellV2 contact refinement and its limits are documented in the [bucket model](bucket-shell.md) and [mount checkpoint](../playtests/2026-10-23-bucket-mount.md). Small-clod inertia uses scale-independent diagonalization; only these clods opt out of the angular-speed clamp and use the incremental gyro correction. The validity guard bounds actual translational plus rotational point speed rather than rejecting physically small clods at an arbitrary angular-rate cutoff. Derived inertia caches are excluded from serialization; a warmed ten-step world and a cold restored world produce identical subsequent saves. No dynamic contact history is hidden in those caches.

Substrate diagnostics use the exact two triangles of each pinned heightfield cell, independently compared with collision raycasts; bilinear height can differ from the physical surface. The V2 world verifies near-touch manifold repair and uses rigid contact stiffness to prevent the prior soft-contact escape path. Full-cycle and delayed-input controls check every clod's highest vertex against that triangular surface. The [coupled free-rotation update](soil-rotation.md) corrects the measured principal phase lag and checks asymmetric refinement. Bulk step/resolution convergence, fast-contact collision timing, wet/cohesive failure, continuous activation, compaction and full machine energy closure remain open.

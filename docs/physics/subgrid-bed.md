The terrain bed now has two counted parts per class: geometric thickness in f32 and a positive remainder of reference bulk volume below the projected geometry's precision. Class order is sediment, overburden, gravel and paydirt. Canonical volume is `thickness * cell_area + bed_remainder_m3`. The remainder is actual material: inventories, dry mass, pore capacity, excavation, deposition and saves include it. It is separate from the signed numerical exchange-feedback array.

This matters when a water film contains less sediment than a large float layer can receive. Rejecting that deposit leaves an artificial suspended tail; discarding it loses material. The integrated mixed-column deposition balance instead chooses the new representable concentration, transfers the removed volume into the two-part bed and retains the small component explicitly. Deposition applies at every positive depth. The visible terrain and flow grid remain 25 cm; this is material precision, not finer physical excavation geometry.

GPU arithmetic orders the high/low sums by magnitude and anchors cancellation through decoded binary mantissas. A fused product residual alone was insufficient: fast-math reassociated the surrounding residual expression, and subtracting a large deposit from a much smaller old layer also lost its contribution. Independent real-GPU controls compare the production helper with f64 transferred volumes. The existing 20,000-stage cyclic material limits remain one ppm of initial mobile material, excluding background bed.

Water retains its existing CFL substeps. [Residence-weighted deposition](residence-transport.md) now operates during their material transport, while scour retains its final immutable snapshot/candidate/reconciliation stages and existing thresholds. Quiet columns use their analytic concentration decay directly. Fluid pressure, FLIP/APIC, DEM, riffles, water-grid resolution and timestep/CFL policy are unchanged; updated bed geometry can change subsequent coupled water trajectories.

The GPU remainder stores four f32 thicknesses, costing 256 KiB at 128×128 and the same extra readback payload. Host state stores f64 volumes and retains positive upload-conversion residue. After collapse or scour reduces a layer's precision scale, an oversized remainder is folded into geometry before rebuilding the water surface. Remaining subgeometry stock stays owned at its cell; its subcell redistribution is not resolved by the coarse collapse model.

Cuts and fills use canonical class quantities rather than dropping precision below a geometric decrement. The existing fixed layer order remains: newly deposited gravel still joins the paydirt channel beneath overburden. This precision correction does not solve arbitrary fill stratigraphy, failure wedges, compaction or fine bucket contact. Those remain local-soil requirements.

The GPU-to-World bridge rounds the visible water surface downward, placing any unrepresentable positive water in the existing counted free-water queue. That queue now also owns horizontal momentum. Release mixes the queued momentum into represented water; velocity itself remains f32, so momentum mixing is accurate to its rounding precision rather than exact f64. Outlet impacts use this same finite-volume receiver. Clean inflow, queue release and intake preserve four-class mobile stock through concentration changes.

A separate positive `suspended_remainder_m3` owns actual mobile class volume below f32 concentration precision. Canonical mobile volume is `C * represented_water_volume + remainder`. It is included in material inventories and pump samples. Unlike the bed remainder it does not change pore capacity. Subprecision stock stays at its cell until it can rejoin represented concentration; its sub-ULP advection is not independently resolved. If no water depth can be represented, its solid inventory strands into the counted bed. This avoids infinite concentrations and invisible loss at the surface-height boundary. These are precision stores, not a soil infiltration or sediment calibration law.

Precision material and queued momentum were introduced in save version 5; ordinary version 6 also preserves machine dynamics; articulated validation writes version 7. Versions 2–4 load with zero absent precision stock and momentum and unchanged inventories; a legacy-version file carrying positive new stock is rejected. Existing moisture and terrain-clock migration rules remain. Quantities must be finite, nonnegative, correctly sized and bounded by the corresponding geometric/concentration precision. Older binaries reject version 5 rather than silently lose this material.

Trace-gold exchange observes canonical paydirt change and the remaining suspension. A controlled quiet production sheet checks deposition and live restore locally. [Coarse gold provenance](terrain-gold.md) now follows actual water substeps and separate bed phases, with the current stationary-pay collapse assumption audited. This is not proof of calibrated separation, arbitrary gold-bearing soil failure or conservative interphase momentum.

This foundation supports thin-film sediment custody before adding soil-water sinks. It does not implement an infiltration law, continuous wet/dry shoreline convergence, evaporation, under-1-mm rough-bed scour, hindered settling, flocculation, or calibrated mud rheology. Prescribed dry/rewet and uniform-column tests are numerical controls, not a complete settling-pool or soil-water model.

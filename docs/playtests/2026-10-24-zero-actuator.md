# Zero-force actuator correction and corrected energy evidence

A zero-stiffness/zero-damping actuator previously remained an enabled Rapier velocity motor. It locked a nominally free hinge. The corrected constructor and live update remove that motor row when both coefficients or the torque cap vanish, and re-enable it when force terms return. Hinge limits, inertia, contacts and solver settings remain intact.

An independent 2 kg, 1 m cube control applies 0.5 Nm at 240 Hz. Its expected rate is 0.006250000326 rad/s; the old implementation produces about 5.82e-11, and the correction produces 0.006250000559. Three axes, spring/damping/zero-cap cases, live disabling/re-enabling and exact restored free-machine continuation pass. No physics acceptance bounds are widened.

The previous “free hinge” energy comparison was invalid because those motors remained enabled. Its raw results are retained, but actuator attribution is withdrawn. Corrected diagnostics attempt 32 cases: 27 complete and five refuse their state at existing angular limits. Truncated prefixes are not complete-window comparisons. The ground and full 1,040-clod bank controls retain every clod and the original stepping parameters.

An independent f64 sum over all 1,045 dynamic bodies measures 3,584.1036867 J, versus the reported 3,584.1039693 J. The saved boundary reproduces the next complete machine/soil and body state exactly. These diagnostics verify reproduction; **the passivity check fails**. The diagnostic 0.01 J threshold is not a calibrated whole-bank tolerance.
